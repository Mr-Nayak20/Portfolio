01 / A WAY OF LOOKING

We work at the edge
of the familiar.

The ordinary is full of things we’ve stopped seeing. A surface. A shadow. A moment between two frames.

The Substance is a fictional visual studio exploring what happens when image, material and motion meet. An experiment in looking again.
