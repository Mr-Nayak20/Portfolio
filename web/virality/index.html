A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Is this connected to a live AI model?

This portfolio version demonstrates the frontend locally. The idea studio uses sample structures, not a live AI model.

Where do my saved ideas go?

Your saved ideas stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded or shared.

Will this make my content go viral?

No tool can promise that. This concept focuses on making the process of thinking, drafting and editing more useful.
