Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This note covers nayakandco.in and this portfolio. I am Shivansh Nayak, writing from India. For anything about your data, email shivansh15nayak@gmail.com.

What this site collects

The contact sheet and the project sheet do not send data to a server. Send opens your own email app, with the words you typed in the message. If you send that email, I receive it like any other email, and I keep it long enough to reply and to talk about the work.

The voice tool stores a note in this browser’s localStorage. It does not leave the machine unless you copy it out.

What this site does not do

There is no account, no advertising pixel, and no sale of a list. Analytics are not running on this portfolio.

Tools

Brief, Tone, and Sight download a model into your browser. The text or image you give them is processed on your machine. A model file may be fetched from a public model host the first time, which means that host can see a request for the file, not the contents of your note.
