I’m Shivansh Nayak.

I work on the part people see and feel: the hierarchy of a page, the rhythm of a scroll, the response of a button and the way an idea comes to life in the browser.

I’m drawn to work that asks for both imagination and care. The visual idea matters. So do the loading state, the mobile experience and what happens when something goes wrong.
