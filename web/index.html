THE PROBLEM

A blank page is rarely a shortage of ideas. It’s the work of choosing an angle, adapting it to a platform and finding words that still sound like you.

THE BUILD

A redesigned landing page leads into a working idea-studio interface. Topic, platform and tone controls produce sample outlines; copying, saving and clearing ideas work locally in the browser.

BEHIND THE INTERFACE

The showcase focuses on responsive layout, typography, component states and a complete interaction flow. The live frontend includes a local idea library, billing-period controls, accessible forms and expandable FAQs.
